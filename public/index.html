Features
A full studio, in your browser
🪐
AI background removal
One click removes any background. AI detects edges precisely, then replaces with official white, blue, or any colour you need.
🎚
Studio-grade adjustments
Brightness, contrast, saturation, and exposure sliders. Seven filters. Auto-enhance and white balance for perfect skin tones.
📐
Every country standard
India, US, UK, Schengen, China, Canada, PAN card, Aadhaar all preloaded. Or enter your own size in millimetres.

Print sheet generator
Arrange multiple photos on one sheet. Set rows, columns, gap size. Download and print at any studio or at home.

Straighten tilted photos instantly. Flip horizontal or vertical. Full undo and redo history.
