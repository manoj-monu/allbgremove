Important Biometric Quality Guidelines

Facial Expression & Eyes

Both eyes must be completely open and looking straight into the camera. Keep a neutral, natural expression with mouth closed. Teeth should not be visible.

Lighting & Shadows

Lighting must be uniform across the face. Avoid flash reflections, red-eye, or dark shadows behind the ears or neck. No filters or excessive beauty retouching.

Eyeglasses & Headgear

Eyeglasses (prescription or sunglasses) are strictly prohibited for US, Schengen, and Indian Passport photos. Religious headgear is allowed if the full oval of the face is visible from chin to forehead.
