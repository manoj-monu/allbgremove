30-DAY MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE FOR PHOTO REJECTIONS

We stand behind our compliance algorithms. If your downloaded passport or visa photo is officially rejected by any government embassy, passport authority, or consular agency due to sizing, background color, or formatting errors, we guarantee a 100% full refund within 30 days of purchase.

1. How to Claim Your Rejection Refund

To request a refund under our 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee, simply email passportphoto2026@gmail.com within 30 days of purchase with:

  • Your registered account email or transaction ID.
  • A brief note or photo of the rejection slip/reason from the consular or passport agency.

Upon verification, your refund will be processed immediately to your original payment method (bank account, card, or UPI) within 5–7 business days without hassle.

2. Digital Credits & Fair Use

Unused wallet credits can be refunded upon request within 7 days of purchase if no processed downloads have taken place. Free trial credits and promotional coupons have no cash value.

To ensure total customer satisfaction, allbgremove.com provides free previews, watermarked test downloads, and complimentary test credits upon initial signup. Users are strongly urged to test image generation, background removal accuracy, and printing features prior to making any financial top-up.

3. Duplicate Deductions & Failed Orders

If an error occurs where your bank account or card is debited multiple times due to a gateway timeout or network drop, and excess credits were not assigned to your account, please submit your transaction reference to passportphoto2026@gmail.com within 7 days. Once verified against our payment gateway logs, the duplicate debit will be reconciled or reversed to the source account within 5–7 business days.

4. Chargebacks and Disputes

Initiating an unapproved bank chargeback without contacting our support desk constitutes a violation of our Terms of Service. In such cases, the user account and associated wallet balance will be permanently suspended pending dispute investigation.

5. Customer Support Contact

If you have any questions or billing concerns, please contact our support team at passportphoto2026@gmail.com or via our Contact Us page.
