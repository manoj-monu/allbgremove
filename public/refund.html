Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 10, 2026 | Effective Date: October 10, 2026

STRICT NO-REFUND POLICY ON DIGITAL CREDITS

All purchases, wallet credit top-ups, and subscriptions on allbgremove.com are strictly non-refundable once delivered. Because credits are allocated immediately and cloud processing bandwidth is consumed instantly, no refunds, reversals, or cancellations will be issued.

1. Nature of Products & Instant Delivery

allbgremove.com provides purely intangible, digital SaaS services. When you complete a transaction through our payment gateway, the purchased credits are loaded instantly into your account balance. Because digital goods cannot be physically returned or un-consumed, all payments are deemed completely final.

2. Free Trial & Quality Evaluation Prior to Purchase

To ensure total customer satisfaction, allbgremove.com provides free previews, watermarked test downloads, and complimentary test credits upon initial signup. Users are strongly urged to test image generation, background removal accuracy, and printing features prior to making any financial top-up.

3. Duplicate Deductions & Failed Orders

If an error occurs where your bank account or card is debited multiple times due to a gateway timeout or network drop, and excess credits were not assigned to your account, please submit your transaction reference to support@allbgremove.com within 7 days. Once verified against our payment gateway logs, the duplicate debit will be reconciled or reversed to the source account within 5–7 business days.

4. Chargebacks and Disputes

Initiating an unapproved bank chargeback without contacting our support desk constitutes a violation of our Terms of Service. In such cases, the user account and associated wallet balance will be permanently suspended pending dispute investigation.

5. Customer Support Contact

If you have any questions or billing concerns, please contact our support team at support@allbgremove.com or via our Contact Us page.
