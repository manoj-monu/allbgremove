How do Wallet Credits work?

How quickly are credits added to my account?

Credits are delivered digitally and instantly within seconds of successful payment confirmation via UPI, Netbanking, or Debit/Credit Cards.

Yes, absolutely! Our ₹199 and ₹499 plans are specially optimized for Indian cyber cafes, CSC centers, and photography studios with complete commercial print rights.

What happens if a photo processing fails?
