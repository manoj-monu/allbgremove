Our Mission & Technology

Empowering Everyone with Biometric AI

allbgremove.com was founded with a singular purpose: making professional, compliant passport and ID photos instant, affordable, and accessible directly from any web browser.

Who We Are

allbgremove.com is an independent SaaS (Software-as-a-Service) platform providing automated computer vision tools for portrait segmentation, background replacement, head framing, and print-sheet layout generation. We cater to thousands of end-users, photo studio owners, and CSC (Common Service Center) operators daily across India and internationally.

Instant Speed

GPU-accelerated AI models produce cutout results in under 2 seconds.

Privacy Focused

Zero permanent image storage. Ephemeral RAM-based processing.

Print Perfection

True 300 DPI layouts for standard 4x6", 5x7", and 30-photo A4 sheets.

Our Technology

Our cloud processing pipeline incorporates state-of-the-art segmentation networks (RMBG-1.4 / BiRefNet) and portrait super-resolution enhancers (CodeFormer) capable of distinguishing individual hair strands from intricate backgrounds while maintaining natural skin tones.

Coupled with our vector canvas layout engine, users can arrange up to 30 photos with biometric cut lines on an A4 sheet, eliminating the need for complex desktop software like Adobe Photoshop.
