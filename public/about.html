About PassportSnap Pro

PassportSnap Pro was created with a simple mission: to make creating professional, studio-quality passport photos accessible and fast for everyone. Whether you are a home user trying to get a visa photo done, or a cyber cafe owner printing hundreds of photos a day, we have built the ultimate AI tool for you.

Our Technology

We leverage State-of-the-Art Deep Learning models hosted on high-performance cloud GPUs. Our Background Removal is powered by BiRefNet, providing pixel-perfect hair masking. Our Face Enhancement uses GFPGAN to magically restore blurred features and provide a DSLR-like sharpness.

Built For India & The World

With smart auto-fitting algorithms, we ensure you get the absolute maximum number of photos on a standard 4x6 or A4 sheet, maximizing your profit margins.
