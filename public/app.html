Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about creating passport size photos online.

What is the official passport size photo in India (in cm / mm)?

The standard Indian passport photo dimension is 35 mm × 45 mm (3.5 cm × 4.5 cm or 1.38 × 1.77 inches). The face should occupy 70% to 80% of the photo height, centered with both ears and eyes clearly visible against a plain white background.

How can I make a passport size photo on my phone for free?

Simply click Upload Photo on allbgremove.com from your smartphone browser. Our smart AI automatically removes messy backgrounds, replaces it with official white or studio blue, enhances face details, and arranges print sheets (4×6, 12×6, or A4) in seconds.

Which background color is required for Passports, Visas & Exams?

Official Indian Passports, US Visas, and Schengen Visas strictly require a pure white background. Many Indian state exams and institutional IDs accept light blue. allbgremove provides 1-click presets for White, Blue, Red, Sky Blue, and Light Grey.

How many photos can I print on 4×6, 12×6, and A4 paper?

Our print layout tool generates 12 photos on 4×6 inch paper, 40 photos on 12×6 inch paper (10×4 grid) for photo studios and mini-labs, and 30 to 36 photos on A4 paper, printed at sharp 300 DPI resolution.

Can I resize photos for PAN Card, Aadhaar, and SSC / UPSC exams?

Yes! You can choose custom crop dimensions or select presets for PAN Card (25×35 mm), US Visa (2×2 inch), and Indian competitive exams (SSC, UPSC, Railway, Police, IBPS) with compliant dimensions and high print clarity.
