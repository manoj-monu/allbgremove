Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Digital Products and Services

PassportSnap Pro (operated by PDFbazaar / Allbgremove) provides web-based AI digital photo processing, background removal, face enhancement, and printable photo sheet generation software. We do not sell or ship any physical goods or printed photographic papers.

2. Delivery Method & Timeline

  • Instant Online Delivery: All processed photos, single downloads, and print-ready sheets (4x6", 5x7", A4) are delivered instantly and directly within your web browser upon completion of the automated AI processing step.
  • Wallet Credits & Token Top-ups: Digital credits purchased through our payment gateway (Razorpay / UPI / Netbanking / Cards) are credited automatically to your account balance immediately upon successful payment confirmation (typically within 1 to 5 seconds).
  • Download Availability: Generated photos and sheets can be downloaded immediately to your device in high-resolution JPEG or PNG format at 300 DPI print quality.

3. Shipping Fees

Because all services and products provided by PassportSnap Pro are 100% digital and delivered online through our cloud infrastructure, no shipping or handling charges apply to any purchase.

4. Delivery Confirmation and Invoicing

Upon successful payment completion, an electronic receipt/confirmation containing your Payment ID, Order ID, and amount will be displayed on the screen and stored in your account transaction log.

5. Technical Delivery Issues

In the rare event that your payment is debited but your wallet credits do not update within 10 minutes due to bank or network latency, please contact our support team with your Payment ID at support@passportsnappro.com or via our Contact Us page. Our team will verify the payment and credit your account within 24 hours.
